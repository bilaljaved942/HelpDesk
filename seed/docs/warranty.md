# Warranty and Product Care

## Northwind Lifetime Warranty
All Northwind-brand jackets, backpacks and tents carry a **lifetime warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship, such as broken zips, failed seams or delaminating waterproof layers.

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, misuse, or damage from improper washing.

## Footwear warranty
Footwear has a **1-year warranty** from the date of purchase against manufacturing defects, such as soles separating from the upper.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number (or proof of purchase) and photos of the problem. Our team reviews every claim. If approved, we repair the item, replace it, or give store credit if it is no longer made.

## Repairs
We repair zips, buckles and small tears on Northwind products for free during the warranty, and for a small fee after that.

## Caring for waterproof jackets
Wash on a cool (30 °C / 86 °F) gentle cycle with a technical wash liquid. Do not use fabric softener or bleach. Tumble dry on low heat to restore the water-repellent finish.

## Caring for down jackets
Wash with a down-specific detergent and tumble dry on low with two clean tennis balls to restore the loft.
