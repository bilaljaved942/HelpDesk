# Contact and Store Information

## Support hours
Our human support team is available **Monday to Friday, 8 am – 6 pm Pacific time**. The AI support assistant answers questions 24/7 and passes anything it cannot solve to the team.

## Our store
Northwind Outfitters Flagship Store, 1200 Pine Street, Portland, Oregon. Open daily 10 am – 7 pm. Online orders can be returned in the store.

## About us
Northwind Outfitters has made outdoor clothing and gear since 2009. We plant one tree for every order through our partnership with a reforestation charity.
