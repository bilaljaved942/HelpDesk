# Payments, Orders and Accounts

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We also offer Klarna for orders over $100, letting you pay in 4 interest-free instalments.

Your card is charged when the order is placed.

## Prices and taxes
All prices are in US dollars. Sales tax is added at checkout for US orders, based on the delivery address.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order. Discount codes cannot be applied to gift cards or to orders that were already placed.

## Price match
If an item you bought goes on sale on our website within 14 days of your purchase, contact support and we will refund the difference once.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order within **1 hour** of placing it, as long as it has not been packed. After that, the order cannot be cancelled, but you can return it once it arrives.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are digital, are sent by email, and never expire. They can be used only on our US website. Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Your account
You can check out as a guest or create an account. With an account you can see your order history and save addresses. To reset your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. Support staff can never see your password.
