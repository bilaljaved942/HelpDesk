# Shipping Policy

Northwind Outfitters ships outdoor clothing and gear from our warehouse in Portland, Oregon.

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2 pm Pacific time on a business day are packed the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and US holidays, are packed the next business day.

## Shipping options and delivery times (United States)
- **Standard shipping:** 4–6 business days. Free on orders of $75 or more; otherwise $6.95.
- **Express shipping:** 2–3 business days. $14.95.
- **Next-day shipping:** 1 business day. $29.95. Must be ordered before 12 pm Pacific time.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International delivery takes 7–14 business days and costs a flat $24.95. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer when the parcel arrives. We do not ship to other countries at this time.

## Tracking your order
When your order ships you receive an email with a tracking number. You can also ask our support assistant for the status of an order using your order number.

## Lost or delayed parcels
If your tracking has not updated for 7 days, or your parcel is more than 5 business days late, contact support. We will open an investigation with the carrier and, if the parcel is lost, send a free replacement or give a full refund.

## Changing your shipping address
You can change the address only before the order ships. Once an order has shipped we cannot redirect it.
