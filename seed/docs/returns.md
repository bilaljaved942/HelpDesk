# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have the original tags attached.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Underwear, socks and swimwear, for hygiene reasons
- Items marked "Final sale"
- Gift cards
- Personalised or monogrammed items

## Footwear
Shoes and boots can be returned within 30 days if they were worn indoors only and the soles are clean. Shoes with outdoor wear cannot be returned, but may be covered by our warranty if they are faulty.

## How to start a return
1. Contact support with your order number.
2. We email you a prepaid return label (free for US orders).
3. Pack the items securely and drop the parcel at any UPS location.

International customers pay their own return shipping.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** after the return reaches our warehouse. Original shipping costs are not refunded, unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

Refunds are always reviewed by a member of our support team. The support assistant cannot issue refunds itself.

## Exchanges
We do not do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the size or colour you want.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or we sent the wrong item, contact support within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping costs.
